## Short description (draft)

Reimagine Indian homes, explore room styles, and plan indicative renovation costs.

## Full description (draft)

See new possibilities for the place you call home. DreamSpace AI helps you explore interior and exterior design ideas from your own photos, organize projects, and plan early renovation budgets.

• Upload a room or house photo and choose a design mode, style, colour palette, and budget guide.
• Compare your original photo with an AI-generated design concept.
• Save designs and organize favourites in My Projects.
• Create a downloadable PDF with project preferences and images.
• Estimate indicative material, labour, transport, tax allowance, and contingency costs in INR using room measurements.
• Edit quantities and assumed unit rates to explore different scenarios.

AI images are creative concepts and may not preserve every room detail. Cost estimates are planning allowances, not contractor quotes or tax advice. Confirm designs, measurements, materials, structural safety, and prices with qualified local professionals.

Photo uploads are sent to DreamSpace AI's server and an AI image processing provider to create a redesign. Account users can delete saved data from Profile settings. See the published privacy policy for providers, retention, and contact details.

**Before publishing:** paid plans are not active in this build. Remove plan/price promises unless billing and entitlements are implemented. Replace the privacy policy text with counsel-approved final terms and add a real support contact.
